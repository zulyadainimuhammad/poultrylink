# PoultryLink Product Vision

## Vision
Build a trusted digital platform that makes Nigeria's poultry value chain easier to access, navigate and understand — connecting farmers, buyers, suppliers and service providers while progressively turning structured market activity into useful intelligence.

## Problem space
Poultry-market participants may face fragmented discovery and coordination. Buyers may need to communicate requirements repeatedly, sellers may struggle to make supply visible, and market information may be difficult to compare when attributes, units, locations and prices are inconsistent. These are hypotheses to validate with participants, not quantified market findings.

## Product proposition
1. **Market access:** structured listings and discovery.
2. **Demand coordination:** buyer requests and seller responses.
3. **Market intelligence:** useful summaries from reliable, permissioned marketplace data.

Commerce, trust services, logistics integrations and AI can expand the platform after foundational workflows are proven.

## Primary users
Poultry farmers and farms; buyers such as traders, retailers, processors, restaurants and institutions; input suppliers; poultry service providers; and administrators.

## Principles
Mobile-first, low-friction workflows; trust and transparency; structured data without burdensome entry; Nigeria-first but geographically extensible; clear distinction between advertised availability and confirmed transactions; measured intelligence rather than unsupported AI claims.

## Success direction
The first proof of value is repeated, useful buyer–seller interaction: quality listings, relevant requests, timely responses and observable next-step outcomes.
