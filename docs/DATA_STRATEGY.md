# Data Strategy

## Domains
Participants and roles; product catalogue and units; supply listings and availability; buyer requests; seller responses; conversation metadata; confirmed transactions only where a reliable confirmation process exists; geography and market references.

## Do not conflate
- **Listing price:** seller-advertised amount at a time.
- **Buyer budget:** buyer's stated range.
- **Response price:** offer proposed in a response.
- **Transaction price:** amount linked to a transaction confirmed under an agreed mechanism.
- **Availability:** seller-reported status, not independent proof of stock.

## Quality controls
Controlled categories/units; valid currency codes; positive quantities and product-specific validation; timestamped changes; stable location IDs; explicit unknown/nulls; duplicate detection; provenance and source metadata.

## Intelligence readiness
Potential views include listing-price distributions, supply counts, request volumes, response rates and time-to-response. These describe platform-observed activity, not necessarily the whole market. State coverage and data limitations.

## Responsible use
Explain data use and obtain appropriate permissions. Aggregate and suppress small groups where identification is possible. Restrict commercially sensitive information. Do not expose identifiable activity without clear lawful basis and transparency. Document metric definitions and limitations.
