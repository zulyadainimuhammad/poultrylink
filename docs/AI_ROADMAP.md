# AI and Machine Learning Roadmap

AI is a later capability, not a substitute for good workflows and data. Start with descriptive analytics and transparent rules.

## Stage 0: structured operations
Consistent taxonomy/units, reliable listing/request/response events, geographic dimensions, timestamps and provenance, clear separation of advertised and confirmed prices.

## Stage 1: descriptive intelligence
Listing activity, supply snapshots, buyer request trends, seller response rates/time, regional comparisons with caveats and historical advertised-price distributions.

## Stage 2: rules-based matching
Rank supply/request candidates using product, geography, quantity, timing and response history. Explain suggestions; do not imply a guarantee.

## Stage 3: predictive research
Potential work: regional demand forecasting, price-range estimation with uncertainty, relevance/ranking and supply-demand gap indicators. Define targets, grain, leakage controls, baselines, data coverage, fairness checks, drift monitoring and human review first.

## Stage 4: personalisation
Explore only with clear value, privacy review and user controls.

## Governance
Reproducible datasets/features, experiment and model version tracking, baseline comparison, uncertainty reporting, monitoring, rollback and human oversight. Never represent predictions as guaranteed prices or fulfilment.
