# Product and Engineering Roadmap

These stages indicate sequencing, not committed delivery dates.

## Stage 0 — Foundation
Repository, product scope, architecture, API/frontend scaffolds, CI and testing baseline.

## Stage 1 — MVP 1: market access
Authentication; profiles/roles; catalogue; listing lifecycle/media; search; buyer requests; seller responses; contextual messaging; moderation essentials.

## Stage 2 — Trust and operations
Contact verification; reviewed seller-verification model; reporting/moderation/audit; notifications; user feedback; pilot improvements.

## Stage 3 — Commerce
Orders, provider payments after legal/security/reconciliation review, delivery partners, cancellation/refund/dispute processes and transaction confirmation.

## Stage 4 — Market intelligence
Activity dashboards, advertised-price trends, regional comparisons, supply/demand indicators and coverage disclosures.

## Stage 5 — AI assistance
Transparent matching improvements, forecasting and recommendations only where sufficient data and governance exist.

Prioritise validated user pain and operational readiness over technical novelty.
